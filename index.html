Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions✤

What is your typical turnaround time?

For custom websites, delivery is typically within 7 days. For SaaS or Enterprise applications, the timeline depends on the exact technical requirements, but a standard MVP usually takes 3 to 6 weeks.

Do you provide ongoing support after deployment?

Absolutely. I offer monthly retainers for ongoing maintenance, proactive security updates, performance monitoring, and content updates to ensure everything scales smoothly.

What stack do you predominantly use?

I build using modern, highly scalable technologies. My frontend stack includes Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, and GSAP for animations. On the backend, I specialize in Node.js, Express, Python, MongoDB, and SQL databases, integrated with leading AI wrappers.

Will my website be SEO optimized?

Yes. All projects are built from the ground up prioritizing Core Web Vitals, server-side rendering parameters, extremely clean markup, schema tags, and optimized asset delivery for maximum indexability.
